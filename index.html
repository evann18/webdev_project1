A Quick Background on Evan Nickolaou

Hello, my name is Evan Nickolaou, and this is going to be a relatively short background on myself. Mainly, this will be about where I went to school before IIT, and my interests in life so far.

2018-2022: Hinsdale South High School

I attended Hinsdale South High School in Darien, IL from 2018 to 2022. At this school, I started my IT career by taking a class known as "Technology Support Internship", or TSI for short.

In TSI, I was able to learn about the fundamentals of technology repairs, troubleshooting, and "customer service" through helping other students and fixing their Chromebooks.

In my second year of TSI as an Honors student, I was able to learn a little about HTML through a Codecadamey course, which was my first introduction to HTML coding. I also took an introductory computer science class which was taught in JavaScript.

2022-2025: College of DuPage

I originally went to the College of DuPage after highschool since I was uncertain about my future, and wanted to save money.

At COD, I was able to take CIS-1310 which was an introductory class on HTML and CSS. This class is where I gained most of my current knowledge about HTML, CSS, and Web Development

2025-2028: Illinois Institute of Technology

Upon completion of my Associate's degree, I transferred to the Illinois Institute of Technology in the Fall of 2025.

At IIT, I am currently taking ITMD-361, a class teaching the fundamentals of web development. It is for this class that I am making this website for Project 1!
