My Fascination With Music

I really love music. Music has been an important part of my life, for basically my entire life. Despite pursuing an education and career in IT, I really enjoy listening to music, learning instruments (currently teaching myself guitar), and finding new songs.

Music as an art fascinates me due to how unique a song can be while also being so connected to everything else in so many different ways. There are so many different genres of music out there, and around 8 billion people on the planet who have different thoughts and ideas.

This "concept" of the connection, yet variability of music is best described by this short video about the song "Misirlou" by Instant Noodle on YouTube below. (Source.)

Some of my favorite musicians/artists/bands

While I do have a preference for certain genres of music, I do listen to, and appreciate, a wide variety of music. I also have a passion for finding relatively new or unknown musicians.

Here is a quick list of who I would consider to be my top 5 favorite musicians (and their respective genres) currently:

  1. Modern Baseball - Midwest Emo
  2. Fox Stevenson - Drum & Bass
  3. High Tea - Folk
  4. Oddisee - Rap
  5. Harrison Gordon - Punk Rock/Emo

Overall, I really enjoy music. In another life, I would have probably tried to pursue an education or career in music. For now, however, I will continue to appreciate and love all kinds of music from anywhere in the world.
